About Me

I am a Research Professor at the Institute of Fisheries Science, Chonnam National University (CNU), Yeosu, South Korea. My research expertise spans environmental toxicology, eDNA metabarcoding, microplastics, PFAS, heavy metal contamination, and marine ecosystem dynamics.

With over 60 peer-reviewed publications in international journals, my research has contributed significantly to understanding the impacts of emerging contaminants including microplastics, PFAS, heavy metals, and tire wear particles on marine and freshwater ecosystems. I have been recognized as a World's Top 2% Scientist by Stanford University and Elsevier (2023) in the field of Biotechnology and Environmental Science.

Research Focus

My research investigates the intersection of environmental pollution, ecotoxicology, and sustainable solutions. Key areas include:

Microplastics & PFAS Toxicology

Investigating the toxicity mechanisms, bioaccumulation patterns, and health effects of microplastics, nanoplastics, PFAS, and tire wear particles in aquatic organisms and human health. Published high-impact reviews in Archives of Toxicology (IF: 10.90), Journal of Hazardous Materials (IF: 10.60), and Water Research (IF: 12.80).

eDNA Metabarcoding & Marine Biodiversity

Applying Next Generation Sequencing and metabarcoding approaches to monitor biodiversity and ecosystem health in Korean aquatic environments including Seomjin River Estuary, Gwangyang Bay, Juam Lake, and Yedang Reservoir. Using QIIME2, DADA2, and R for bioinformatics analysis.

Heavy Metals & Environmental Monitoring

Studying heavy metal bioaccumulation in benthic macroinvertebrates of Korean streams and marine environments using ICP-MS and DGT (Diffusive Gradients in Thin Films). Assessing seasonal impacts of monsoon on heavy metal distribution in aquatic ecosystems.

Molecular Responses to Environmental Stressors

Studying cellular and molecular responses to environmental contaminants in model organisms including zebrafish and aquatic invertebrates, using transcriptomics and computational approaches to understand toxicity mechanisms and develop molecular biomarkers.

Biofabricated Nanomaterials

Developing eco-friendly nanoparticles and assessing their potential in environmental remediation, antimicrobial applications, and as alternatives to conventional chemicals in various environmental applications.
